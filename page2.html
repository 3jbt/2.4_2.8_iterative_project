Rangituhi Walk

DATE BY HOUSE:

9 DEC

10 DEC

7 DEC

8 DEC

Traverse the peaks of the hills surrounding Tawa - enjoy these exhilarating views with your classmates!

What to wear: Active clothes - no denim please!

What to bring: A packed lunch, water, jacket, good attitude!

View from Rangituhi

About the Activity:

Students are transported to the trailhead via private bus. Teachers will accompany & assist students as they navigate the path towards the lookout & the summit of Rangituhi / Colonial Knob. After this point, the group will traverse the tops of several hills, then arriving at Te Ngahere o Tawa.

During this activity, students will learn how to:

  • Read trail maps
  • Observe trees & leaves
  • Locate places of interest from various vantage points along the route.

There is a $50 prezzy card as a prize for the range of activities that students can complete along the way.

Students will then walk back to Tawa College upon completion of the trail, where they will be dismissed. This should happen by 3pm at the latest.
