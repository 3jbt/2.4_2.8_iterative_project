Adrenalin Forest & Te Rauparaha Arena

DATE BY HOUSE:

9 DEC

10 DEC

11 DEC

12 DEC

Students will get to climb amongst the treetops in Adrenalin Forest before returning to Te Rauparaha Arena Aquatic Centre for some fun in the pool.

What to wear: Active clothes - no denim please!

What to bring:

  • Packed lunch
  • Water
  • Jacket
  • Swimming gear
  • A good attitude!

About the Activity:

Students will catch the 8:28am train from Tawa station, alight at Porirua, and then walk as a group to Adrenalin Forest. Following the safety briefing, students have 3 hours to climb and test themselves.

After having some lunch we will leave Adrenalin Forest and walk to Te Rauparaha Arena, where students have use of the whole of the main pool, spas, sauna, steam room, hydroslide and deep water swimming lane.

If students are not swimming, there is an outside area off the side of the main pool where they can relax.

If you indicated on the permission form that students can leave by themselves directly from the pool (as they live in the local area), they will be dismissed from here at around 2.15pm.

The rest of the group will walk back to Porirua station and catch the 2:39pm train back to Tawa station where they will be dismissed.

If it is absolutely impossible for your child to catch this 8:26am train, one teacher will stay behind and accompany anybody left behind on the next train. This is a last resort, as these students will not be able to take full advantage of the three hours allocated for the first half of this activity.
