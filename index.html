Activity Timetables

In the Year 10 EOTC Programme, students partake in 4 action-packed days with various activities. The focus of this programme is about citizenship & social awareness of the students.

EOTC takes place from Tuesday 3 December to Friday 6 December. All activities commence at Tawa College past 8AM and return to school grounds by 3:20PM. Starting times vary by activity - see the activity pages.

Community Service

It is expected that students will wear clothes suitable for movement for all activities. As activities will last for a whole school day, it's a good idea to bring lunch as usual for the activities.

Packing requirements will vary by activity. Specific packing lists can be found at the top of each activity page, and a full packing list can be found below. ⇓ If there are any difficulties supplying these required items, please contact us so we can discuss a suitable solution.

Additional Items to Pack

For each of the activity days, students must bring:

  • Packed lunch & water to drink
  • Footwear & clothing suitable for movement
  • Any medicines required for consumption
  • Jumper or rain jacket, subject to the weather

For Camp Wainui and Adrenalin Forest & Pool:

  • Swimming gear (togs, towel, goggles, wet bag, etc.)

The trip to Camp Wainui lasts 2 days and involves several more activities, which require additional items to be brought. See the Camp Wainui page for more details.

  • Two changes of clothes, for overnight & the second day.
  • Snacks & games for overnight entertainment
  • Sleeping bag
  • A day pack containing extra water & snacks for the Orongorongo River Walk

Students will be sleeping in tents with their friends at Camp Wainui.

If your family can provide a tent, please let their Mauri Tau teacher know.

Payment Information

There is a $90 fee for participating in the EOTC Programme. This is all-inclusive of transport, private hiring of venues, et cetera. It can be paid to the school bank account (12-XXXXXX-XXXXX-XX) or in person at the school office.

If you may have any difficulties with this payment, don’t hesitate to reach out so that we can figure out a solution that fits your needs.

General Reminders

Individual safety is paramount during the EOTC Programme. Teachers will accompany & supervise students during the activities. Students are required to act in a manner that does not endanger themselves or others. Violation of these guidelines will result in the removal of the student from the activity, and they will be sent home at the expense of their parents or caregivers.

On all of the trips, students must abide by the school rules. This includes absolutely no cigarettes, vapes, drugs, or alcohol to be consumed or brought. Anyone found non-compliant will be removed from the activity and sent home at the expense of their parents/caregivers.
